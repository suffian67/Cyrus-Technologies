When incidents happen

Calm under
pressure.

A security incident creates more than a technical problem. It creates uncertainty across leadership, operations, legal, communications, and the people responsible for getting the business moving again.

Our role is to bring structure to that uncertainty. We help establish what happened, determine what is affected, contain the threat, and guide the organization toward recovery.

The response lifecycle

Move from
chaos to control.

01

Detect & understand

Establish the facts quickly. Determine what happened, what systems are involved, and whether the threat is still active.

02

Contain the threat

Limit the attacker’s ability to cause further harm while protecting evidence and maintaining critical business operations.

03

Investigate deeply

Trace activity, identify affected assets, understand the attack path, and build a reliable picture of the incident.

04

Recover with confidence

Restore affected environments safely and validate that the organization can return to normal operations.
